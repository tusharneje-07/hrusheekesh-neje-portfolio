01 Overview

Bringing Local Pharmacies Online

Biddano Quick Commerce is a hyper‑local mobile marketplace that connects neighbourhood pharmacies with customers who need medicines at short notice. The product aims to give small, independent pharmacies a digital sales channel that rivals large e‑commerce players, while keeping the ordering experience fast, trustworthy and affordable.

02 The Problem

Low‑Tech, High‑Friction Ordering

Local pharmacies still rely on phone calls, WhatsApp chats, and handwritten order books. This leads to:

  • Lost orders and duplicate requests
  • Opacity on pricing, stock availability and delivery ETA
  • Trust gaps – customers fear counterfeit or expired medicines
  • Time‑consuming manual inventory & bookkeeping
03 Research & Discovery

Understanding local pharmacy pain points

We conducted in‑depth interviews with 12 independent pharmacy owners across three tier‑2 cities, shadowed ordering processes, and ran a survey of 150 end‑customers to map the end‑to‑end buying journey.

Research Methods

  • On‑site observation of order taking
  • Phone/WhatsApp interview recordings
  • Competitive audit of regional delivery apps
  • Quantitative survey on ordering frequency & price sensitivity

Key Insights

  • Trust is the biggest barrier – customers doubt product authenticity.
  • Speed matters – average order fulfillment > 48 h.
  • Pricing transparency drives repeat orders.
  • Pharmacies need a lightweight inventory view on mobile.
04 Design Decisions

Designing a fast, trustworthy mobile flow

Before – Pain Points
  • No clear product image – users can’t verify authenticity.
  • Manual address entry on each order – adds friction.
  • Pricing hidden in text – no quick comparison.
  • Order status only via phone call – no visibility.
After – Design Solutions
  • High‑resolution product thumbnails with verification badge.
  • Saved address book and auto‑complete for checkout.
  • Transparent price table with discount tiers.
  • Real‑time order tracking timeline within the app.
05 Visual Language

A calming, trustworthy UI for quick commerce

The visual system blends a soft teal‑blue palette with generous whitespace, signalling reliability while keeping the experience snappy.

Color Palette

Primary teal, complementary warm orange for CTAs, and muted neutrals for background keep focus on product images and pricing.

Typography

Agrandir for headings, Manrope for body – high‑contrast, legible at a glance on small screens.

08 Reflections

Key learnings from the quick‑commerce journey

01

Trust is earned visually

Verified badges, clear product photos and transparent pricing reduced perceived risk dramatically.

02

Speed wins repeat business

Reducing order‑to‑delivery time to under 2 minutes on‑screen kept users coming back.

03

Mobile‑first inventory matters

A lightweight stock view on the phone empowered pharmacists to manage SKUs without a desktop.

04

Data informs iteration

Early analytics on order funnels guided rapid UI tweaks that lifted conversion by 20%.
